Lion's Pride Rx

An online telehealth portal for men's prescription needs. Simple, affordable and secure.

Overview

Lion's Pride Rx is a telehealth platform that lets men get prescription treatments 100% online. Customers choose a treatment, complete a short online health evaluation that a US-licensed physician reviews, and have FDA-approved medication shipped discreetly from a US-based pharmacy. No insurance is needed. The platform covers sexual health and hair loss treatments, with testosterone, weight loss and custom prescriptions on the way.
