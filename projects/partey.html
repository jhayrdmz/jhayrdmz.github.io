Partey

A real-time multiplayer party game platform. Games on the big screen, played on your phone.

Overview

Partey turns any room into a game night. One device hosts, a TV or tablet becomes the shared display, and everyone plays on their own phone. Nothing to install and no accounts needed. The host creates a room, picks a game mode and a lineup of games, then pairs the display with a 6-digit code. Players join by scanning a QR code or typing the room code, choose a nickname and avatar, and compete for the crown. Speed Quiz, Guess the Majority, Reflex Battle, The Impostor and Draw & Guess are playable today, with more games on the way.
