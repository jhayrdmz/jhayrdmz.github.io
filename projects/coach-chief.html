Coach Chief

A free online booking page for coaches. More bookings, less texting, more money.

Overview

Coach Chief is a booking platform for session-based professionals such as coaches, personal trainers, tutors and instructors. Each coach gets a free booking page where clients see live availability and book instantly, without texting back and forth. Coaches create sessions like private lessons, small-group sessions, clinics and camps, then manage every booking from their own admin area. Paid plans add one-click texting to clients, automated reminders to cut no-shows, online payments (card, Venmo, Zelle and more) and a customizable website with domain hosting.
