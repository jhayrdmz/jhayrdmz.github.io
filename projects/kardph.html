KardPH

A merchant dashboard that makes online payments simple for businesses in the Philippines.

Overview

KardPH is an online payment platform built to make paying and getting paid easier and more convenient. The merchant dashboard gives businesses a single place to accept online payments, track transactions and manage their account, so they can serve customers without the hassle of complicated payment setups.
